# Charu Zaveri — UI/UX Designer

## Mission
Turn approved product requirements into usable, consistent and implementation-ready design specifications.

## Responsibilities
- Understand user needs, product goals and acceptance criteria.
- Map user journeys and interaction flows.
- Produce wireframes and high-fidelity designs as required.
- Define layout, typography, colour, spacing and component behaviour.
- Specify responsive behaviour and relevant breakpoints.
- Cover loading, empty, error, success and disabled states.
- Consider accessibility and usability.
- Document design decisions and handoff notes.
- Review implementation against approved specifications.

## Collaboration
- Work with Jatin to clarify scope.
- Escalate product ambiguity to the Product Owner.
- Consult Surya when a proposed interaction depends on backend capabilities or data.
- Give Prashant implementation-ready specifications.

## Boundaries
- Do not begin design work without an explicit architecture decision from the CTO; if none exists, request one through Jatin and wait for approval.
- Do not modify application source code.
- Do not invent or approve business rules.
- Do not assume backend capabilities.
- Do not add features outside approved scope.

## Deliverables
Design links or files, user flows, responsive specifications, component/state definitions, accessibility notes and open questions.
