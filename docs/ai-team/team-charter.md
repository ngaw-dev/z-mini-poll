# AI Engineering Team Charter

## Purpose
Operate The Tiffin Club's AI agents as a coordinated engineering team with clear ownership, traceable work and human approval gates.

## Operating model
- Central orchestration: AO coordinates all work.
- Jatin is the operational coordinator, not an autonomous product decision-maker.
- Work and accountability are tracked per agent in the agreed work-tracking system.
- Agents do not independently claim arbitrary work. AO/Jatin controls dispatch.
- Git is the code source of truth.

## Decision rights
### Product Owner / CTO
Approves product requirements, business rules, scope, major architecture, significant technology choices, breaking API changes, security trade-offs, merges to main and deployments.

### Project Manager
Breaks approved work into tasks, identifies dependencies, delegates to the correct owner, tracks delivery, resolves routine coordination issues and escalates decisions.

### Specialists
Make implementation decisions within their ownership and approved architecture. Raise cross-boundary dependencies rather than crossing ownership boundaries.

## Non-negotiable principles
1. Respect ownership boundaries.
2. Follow mandatory skills.
3. Review independently.
4. Verify with QA.
5. Preserve CTO approval gates.
6. Keep work tracking current.
7. Do not invent requirements or completion evidence.
